These are cool expansion ideas. Here’s a clear breakdown of what they would mean and how they could fit:

Current project status
We’re still building a nearly accurate Battle Draw clone. The core (drawing armies → watching them fight) is the main goal. We’ve got:
Drawing / erasing / brush size
Teams + free mode
Basic AI + combat
Emojis + size variation
Projectiles + flamethrower

Your new ideas ranked by complexity & fit

| Idea | Difficulty | How well it fits Battle Draw | Notes |
|------|------------|------------------------------|-------|
| Block placing | Medium | Good | Easy to add as a new “Terrain” tool or separate mode. Walls/blocks that units can stand on or be blocked by. |
| Falling sand (water, fire, chemicals, seeds) | High | Excellent potential | This is basically turning it into a Sandboxels-style engine. Very fun, but a big technical jump (cellular automata). |
| Survival (hunger, eating, planting, growing) | High | Medium | Requires resource loops, timers, player character or unit needs. Changes the game from “watch armies fight” to management. |
| Platformer mode | High | Low-Medium | Needs gravity, jumping, solid platforms, player control. Big shift from top-down army sandbox. |
| 3D mode | Very High | Low | Completely different tech (Three.js or similar). Would essentially be a new project. |

Recommended paths forward

Option 1 – Stay focused (recommended for now)  
Keep improving the Battle Draw clone until it feels close to the original (better physics, more special interactions like barrel throwing, better death animations, sound, etc.). Then decide whether to expand.

Option 2 – Hybrid expansion  
Add the easiest high-value features first:
Better block / terrain placing (solid walls that units collide with)
Simple falling-sand elements (water + fire + some reactions)
Keep the army fighting on top of that

Option 3 – Full pivot  
Turn this into a bigger sandbox game with survival + falling sand + armies. This would be a much larger project.
